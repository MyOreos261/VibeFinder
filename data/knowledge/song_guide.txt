ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT
===================
This guide describes every song in the VibeFinder catalog (data/songs.csv) in depth: its sound, vocals, structure, lyric themes, when to play it and when to skip it, how it compares with similar songs, and direct answers to common questions. All songs and artists are fictional and were created for a classroom project. Feature values come directly from the catalog; activity playlists were produced by running the VibeFinder recommender itself, so this guide never contradicts the app.
How VibeFinder uses it: the retriever extracts the text and splits it into one chunk per song, from the line 'Song: <title>' to the line 'End of song: <title>'. When a request names a song ('songs like Storm Runner'), the agent copies that song's genre, mood and energy. When a request names a sound ('piano', 'fiddle', 'no vocals'), songs whose Sound or Vocals lines mention it get a bonus, and the matching sections are passed to the language model as evidence.

CHAPTER 6: GLOSSARY
===================
Energy: How intense and active a song feels, from 0.0 (very calm) to 1.0 (very intense). VibeFinder rewards closeness to the energy you want, not 'higher is better'.
Valence: How positive a song sounds, from 0.0 (sad, dark) to 1.0 (happy, bright).
Danceability: How suitable the rhythm is for dancing, from 0.0 to 1.0.
Acousticness: How acoustic (real instruments, unplugged) versus electronic or produced a song sounds, from 0.0 to 1.0.
Tempo (BPM): Beats per minute. Around 60-80 BPM feels slow, 90-120 moderate, 125+ fast.
Instrumental: A song with no sung or rapped vocals. Marked in this guide as 'Vocals: none (instrumental)'.
Related genre: A genre that fans of another genre usually also enjoy, taken from the VibeFinder genre notes. Related genres earn half of the genre points when ranking.
Mood family: Groups of moods that feel alike: chill/relaxed/focused, happy/euphoric/confident, intense/angry, and sad/melancholy/moody/nostalgic.
Confidence: VibeFinder's own estimate of how well it understood and matched a request (0 to 1). Below 0.5 it asks the listener for more detail.
